# Code of conduct

Be civil and stick to the work. Critique the skill, the benchmark or the code, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not welcome, and maintainers may remove comments or block accounts that do this.

Disagreement about results is normal here: the benchmarks are small and the judge is the author. Bring a prompt, an answer and a measurement and the conversation stays useful.

Report problems by opening an issue marked "conduct" or by contacting a maintainer through their GitHub profile.
